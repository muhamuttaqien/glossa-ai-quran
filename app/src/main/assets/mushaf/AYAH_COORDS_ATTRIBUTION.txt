Adaptation in ayah_bounds.json: the 604 source JSON files were combined by
page, retaining line number, surah number, ayah number, and the original
min/max x/y coordinates as seven-element numeric arrays:
[line, surah, ayah, min_x, max_x, min_y, max_y].
The source's repeated page_number and position fields were omitted.
The boxes for verses 69:1–8 on page 566 were realigned to the bundled image
because that page places the basmala above the first verse line.
The Qur'an page images and Arabic text were not changed.

The interaction maps those coordinates to the bundled compact PNG images.
Long presses close to a verse boundary do nothing because the source
coordinate images and these compact PNGs are not pixel-identical.
The white selection overlay draws rounded areas around the mapped ayah
segments, tightened to their printed ink. It does not alter page artwork.

# Glossa AI — Qur'an Version

Android reader with Uthmani Arabic text (Hafs), English and Indonesian translations, and verse-by-verse murottal from five reciters. The app interface is in English. Its navigation and reading layout take inspiration from Quran for Android; it does not contain that app's images or code.

## Features

- Browse and search all 114 surahs; open any of the 30 juz at its first ayah.
- Read all 604 fixed pages of the Madinah Mushaf (Hafs) or switch to verse-by-verse translations of the ayahs on the same page, including pages spanning multiple surahs. Choose English (the default), Indonesian, or English + Indonesian; long press a mapped ayah on a Mushaf page to open its translation and actions with a translucent black highlight (10%) in light mode or white (24%) in dark mode around its mapped line segments; long presses that do not match an ayah have no effect. Tap the reading area once in either mode to show or hide the top and bottom controls with a gentle slide animation. In Translation, tapping a verse card also toggles the controls while marking that verse as read; its actions remain clickable. In the combined view, English appears first.
- Save verse bookmarks, resume from the last selected or played verse or the last swiped Mushaf page, share a verse, and switch between light and dark reading. Each translation card keeps Listen, Bookmark, and Share in a compact actions menu. Light is the default.
- Choose Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary, Muhammad Siddiq Al-Minshawi, Abdul Rahman Al-Sudais, or Saud Al-Shuraim.
- Listen to an ayah; playback advances through the rest of the surah. In Read Arabic, the current ayah follows the playback with the same mapped highlight used for verse selection; it remains highlighted while paused and clears when playback ends or stops. Previous, pause/resume and next controls are included.
- All Arabic Mushaf pages and ayah hit boxes are bundled. Choosing a surah, juz, Continue reading, or bookmark from the index opens the corresponding page in Read Arabic; a bookmarked ayah is highlighted even while chapter details load. Previously opened surahs, page metadata, juz starting points and audio URL lists are cached; new translations, verse details and audio streams need internet. The selected qari and display mode are remembered.

## Run

Open this directory in Android Studio with JDK 17 and Android SDK 35 installed. Sync Gradle, then run the `app` configuration on an Android 8.0+ device or emulator with internet access. Alternatively, run `./gradlew assembleDebug` after configuring the SDK. The debug APK is written to `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/`.

Validate the page images, verse selection, API and audio on a device before distributing the app.

## Content and rights

The bundled Hafs page artwork comes from the [Quran Images collection](https://github.com/Miftah-Fentaw/Quran-Images), which credits the [Quran.com image generator](https://github.com/quran/quran.com-images) and the Madinah Mushaf. The [King Fahd Qur'an Printing Complex](https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/mushaf-printing) publishes the underlying Madinah Mushaf digital artwork and its usage rights. The original PNGs are fixed page artwork, not Arabic text reflowed to the screen width. The bundled ayah hit boxes are adapted from [GitCreativeCode/quran-ayah-coords](https://github.com/GitCreativeCode/quran-ayah-coords), licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); see `app/src/main/assets/mushaf/AYAH_COORDS_ATTRIBUTION.txt`. The hit boxes add interaction without changing the original artwork. Page verse indices, Indonesian translation (`id.indonesian`), English translation (`en.sahih`, Saheeh International), and streaming audio are loaded over HTTPS from [AlQuran Cloud](https://alquran.cloud/api). The Translation tab displays the bundled King Fahd Qur'an Printing Complex QPC Hafs v22 text with its matching unmodified font, indexed by the API's global ayah number. This gives the verse cards Madinah-style Arabic lettering while their line wrapping remains responsive rather than matching the fixed Mushaf page. The API response supplies audio URLs and both translation edition names. The bundled [Hafs v22 text and font](https://github.com/nuqayah/qpc-fonts/tree/8a4f39d563ea69c994416a1692827e38156c548d/text-mushafs/UthmanicHafs_V22) are copies of the King Fahd Complex materials; the font's embedded distribution terms are reproduced in `design/qpc_hafs_v22-LICENSE.txt`. The script is bundled losslessly as a gzip-compressed JSON file at `app/src/main/assets/qpc_hafs_v22.dat`, and the font is stored unchanged in `app/src/main/res/font/qpc_hafs_v22.ttf`. Its [terms](https://alquran.cloud/terms-and-conditions) require attribution to translators and state that translators and reciters retain their rights. Obtain any permissions required for commercial distribution.

Read Arabic loads only the numbered `1.png`–`604.png` files. The 114 surah-title decorations across 98 pages use a thin rectangular border and the floral motif extracted from the user's supplied reference screenshot, tinted Glossa red (`#EF4D4D`). Its original left and right floral silhouettes are scaled to the printed banner height; the Arabic titles are copied in full from the original Mushaf images within a bounds check that excludes the source frame, except on page 2. The extracted transparent motif is `design/surah-title-floral-mask.png`; it excludes the reference image’s original green border so that only one red border is drawn. Pages 1 and 2 omit the surrounding full-page frame. Page 1 retains its printed Arabic title; page 2 uses the original Al-Baqarah title pixels from the user-supplied full-frame `2.png`, with their anti-aliased edges separated from the printed crest (`design/al-baqarah-title-mask.png`). Their ayat remain from the original Mushaf artwork. The printed verse-marker ornaments throughout all 604 pages are replaced with a thin red circle around the original Arabic numeral pixels. Detached ornament flecks inside the circles are removed while retaining the printed numerals. Text lines, basmalahs, page dimensions, and hit coordinates remain fixed. Dark mode keeps the same `#EF4D4D` red while inverting the Arabic ink; the palette recognizes rounded RGB values along translucent floral edges so they do not invert to a green tint. `tools/simplify_mushaf_artwork.py` reproduces these PNGs from the original artwork at Git commit `aca71eb` using Pillow, NumPy, and OpenCV; `tools/retouch_title_banners.py` updates only the banner decoration on already simplified numbered PNGs. No Arabic is retyped or generated. The source artwork remains available through Git history. The old `-frame` and `-no-frame` generators remain as historical references, but their derivative PNGs are not loaded.

The light reading palette uses warm off-white pages and `#080808` Arabic text. Glossa red `#EF4D4D` appears in the Mushaf ornaments, selected tabs, and the main playback button; neutral text and a muted gold mark the remaining reading content. The index has a compact header and a bordered last-read card, translation cards use fine borders, and the reader header, tabs, page information, qari selector, player, and navigation-bar backdrop use dark surfaces in both reading modes and themes, while the Mushaf page and Translation list remain light in light mode. The optional dark theme uses `#080808` (background), `#101010` (surface), `#171717` (cards), and `#F8F8F8` (text).

The launcher icon uses the supplied 128×128 Glossa AI `G.` artwork. Android density resources are generated at 48, 72, 96, 144, and 192 pixels; the original is kept in `design/glossa-icon-128.png`. A higher-resolution original would be preferable for a future 512×512 Play Store listing.

Read Arabic shows one fixed Mushaf page at a time with the original printed line breaks. Swipe right for the next page or left for the previous page; the adjacent page follows the drag and settles with a short horizontal animation when released. Translation shows the ayahs on that same page, including any surah boundary; its cards scroll vertically, while horizontal swipes do not change the page. The reader header takes the surah name from the bundled page map so it stays stable while page metadata loads. Continue reading records the first ayah on each page reached by a Read Arabic swipe and reopens that page. Short swipes return to the current page. The page number appears when reading controls are shown, without navigation buttons. The Android navigation bar stays dark while reading in either theme, matching the reading controls; the index follows the selected theme. In dark mode, the app remaps the page artwork's colors to bright white ink on a black Mushaf page, preserving red ornaments and the original layout. Long press a mapped ayah on the page to open its translation and actions. Translation verse cards use the bundled QPC Hafs font and script for Arabic, and their touch feedback has no ripple. The action sheet has 4 dp rounded upper corners and shows Listen, Bookmark, Share as plain clickable text without underlines; Bookmark and Bookmarked both use Glossa red; the label changes when saved in the action sheet and Translation card menu; the ayah's mapped line segments receive rounded highlights in translucent black (10%) for light mode or white (24%) for dark mode, tightened around the printed ink while selected. A short tap toggles the dark top and bottom controls, page metadata, and page number with a slide animation; they start hidden in both reading modes and overlay the Mushaf page or translation list without resizing the content in either theme. Long presses near ayah boundaries or outside mapped text have no effect. There is no page verse list in Read Arabic, and the fixed artwork is not selectable word by word. Page metadata and translations are obtained from AlQuran Cloud; Read Arabic can display its bundled page before those details load, while translation cards and verse details require a connection when uncached. The first version plays audio while the app is open. Background playback, audio downloads, gapless playback, custom repeat, and full tafsir are not implemented yet.
